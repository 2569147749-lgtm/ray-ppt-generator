# Presentation Preferences

Use these as defaults only when the current request does not specify otherwise.

## Established preferences

- Prefer original visual systems over copied third-party templates.
- Prefer professional, direct, restrained communication.
- Use real screenshots when presenting a website, application, workflow, or product.
- Prioritize clear business logic and measurable value over decorative storytelling.
- Keep formulas connected to the reasoning and render them in LaTeX when formulas are necessary.
- Do not fabricate results; label demonstration figures explicitly.

## Current visual preference

- Yellow and warm white with dark ink text is an approved direction.
- Editorial typography, strong hierarchy, thin rules, circles, and arrows are acceptable.
- Avoid purple gradients, excessive cards, generic AI aesthetics, and ornamental clutter.

## Density defaults

- Product introductions and interview presentations: low-density, speaker-led.
- Reports and asynchronous reviews: high-density, reading-first.
- When uncertain, prefer speaker-led slides with one main idea per slide.
